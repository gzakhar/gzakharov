Most of what I write doesn't end up anywhere public. Half-finished ticket descriptions, notes to myself about a design decision I've since reversed, drafts of posts that never made it past the second paragraph because the idea turned out to be smaller than it felt at the time.

I used to think of that as waste. Lately I don't.

## The draft is doing something

Writing a thing down, even badly, forces a decision that thinking alone doesn't: does this idea survive being made concrete? A lot of things that feel clear in my head fall apart the moment I try to write a sentence that explains them. That's useful information, and it only shows up on the page.

So the margins &mdash; the notes that never get cleaned up, the drafts that never ship &mdash; aren't wasted effort. They're the filter. What ends up here, on the actual blog, is just whatever survived that process intact.

## Why bother publishing any of it

Because the filter isn't perfect, and an audience, even a small one, is a better editor than I am on my own. Writing for no one lets you get away with vagueness. Writing for someone, even hypothetically, doesn't.

That's the whole reason this page exists.
