Finally shipped a public-facing portfolio. I've been meaning to do this for a while &mdash; having a durable place on the internet that's mine, not a LinkedIn profile or a Twitter thread that disappears into a feed.

The design is heavily inspired by [scf.so](https://scf.so). I liked the radical minimalism &mdash; no hero videos, no parallax scrolling, no "I'm a passionate problem solver." Just content doing the talking. If the work is interesting, the reader stays. If not, they leave &mdash; and that's fine.

## What's here

Two pages, nothing fancy:

- <c>Portfolio</c> &mdash; who I am, what I've built, and where to find me.
- <c>Ideas</c> &mdash; the page you're reading. Occasional posts on what I'm working on, reading, or thinking about.

## Why an ideas page?

I wanted a place to drop longer-form updates without the friction of a newsletter. A newsletter implies a schedule and an audience. This is just a log &mdash; things I'm interested in, libraries I'm using, predictions I'm willing to put in writing.

## Stack

Plain HTML and CSS, now with Jekyll for content separation. Hosted on GitHub Pages. Adding a new post is just creating a markdown file in `_posts/`.
